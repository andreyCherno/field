Everything below is a specification, not a preference. Where a tolerance is given, work to it.

## 1. Garment

| | Spec |
|---|---|
| Style | Short sleeve tee, boxy body, drop shoulder |
| Fabric | 100% cotton, combed / ring-spun, single jersey |
| Weight | **280 gsm** finished (accept 270–290; state the measured value) |
| Yarn | 21s or finer, low-twist, for a dense hand without a rough face |
| Shrinkage | Pre-shrunk / enzyme washed. Max 5% length, 3% width after one 40°C wash |
| Neck rib | 2×2 rib, 20 mm wide finished, self-fabric or matched rib |
| Neck tape | Shoulder-to-shoulder taped, self-fabric |
| Seams | **Side-seamed** (not tubular). Double-needle coverstitch at hem and sleeve |
| Sleeve | Drop shoulder, set-in, hemmed 25 mm |
| Bottom hem | 30 mm, double-needle |
| Labels | **No labels of any kind on the sample.** No brand, no size tab, no care label. Heat-transfer neck label only if I supply the artwork later |
| Thread | Colour-matched to garment, 100% polyester core-spun |

Tubular bodies, single-needle hems and 180–220 gsm fabric are exactly what this
spec is written to avoid. If the 280 gsm quality you stock cannot meet it, say so
before sampling rather than substituting.

## 3. Print

| | Spec |
|---|---|
| Method | **Puff (raised / 3D foam) screen print** |
| Colours | **One.** One screen, one pass. No halftones, no gradients, no underbase colour |
| Placement | Front, centred left-right; top of the print **110 mm below the collar seam at centre front** |
| Size | See `SPEC.txt` in the artwork folder — 190–220 mm wide depending on the design |
| Artwork | 300 dpi transparent PNG at 100% print size, plus vector PDF and a 1-bit separation |
| Hand | Raised and even. No flat patches, no scorching, no cracking when the print is folded double |
| Cure | To the ink manufacturer's schedule. The puff must rise uniformly across the whole image |

**Do not modify the artwork.** Do not re-trace it, thicken it, add an outline, or
rebuild it in your own software. It is drawn for puff expansion already: every
mass is at least 3.2 mm and every gap at least 3.0 mm at the printed size, and
re-tracing destroys that.

| 1 | Black — PANTONE 19-0303 TCX Jet Black | White puff — PANTONE 11-0601 TCX Bright White |

For the off-white, send photographs of your two closest stock colours before
printing. A cream that reads yellow or grey is the single most common way this
colourway fails.

## 5. Before you ship — required photographs

Send all of these and wait for my confirmation before shipping:

1. The **printed sample on your own fabric**, photographed in daylight, flat and
   at an angle so the raised edge is visible. Not a catalogue photo, not a
   similar design.
2. A **close-up of the print edge**, so the puff height and edge quality are visible.
3. The garment **laid flat with a tape measure** across the chest and down the length.
4. A photo of the **inside of the neck and side seam**.
5. The **fabric weight reading** — a photo of the GSM cutter/scale, or a lab report.
