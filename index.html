What it is

Field is a price-comparison engine for streetwear and fashion. It indexes independent boutiques and brand stores across the US, Europe and Israel, and ranks every piece by its true landed cost to the buyer's country: item price, shipping, customs duty, import VAT and card FX — computed per destination, never guessed.

What makes it different

  • Honest ranking. Nothing is ranked by who pays us — only by what it costs delivered.
  • Verified discounts only. A price drop counts only when the retailer's own feed says so.
  • Cross-shop matching. The same item found at multiple retailers, cheapest first, with hard vetoes against false matches.
  • Uncertainty stated, never hidden. When shipping or tax can't be known, the page says so and lowers its confidence.
