New from China and Japan

Forty-five labels that are only starting to be carried outside their home markets — grouped not by what they sell but by what they are trying to do.

Every brand fact here carries a source. Where a founding year, a founder or an official shop could not be established, the card says nothing rather than estimating — an empty field means it was looked for and not found.

Price positions are measured from live items in the catalogue, never taken from a brand's own description of itself. Most of these labels have no items in the catalogue yet, and those carry no position at all.
