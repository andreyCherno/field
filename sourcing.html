Size charts frequently run by body weight rather than chest measurement. Before any repeat order, wash a sample twice — a spliced garment fails where two fabrics shrink at different rates.

Getting it here

Superbuy buys in China, holds the parcel, and forwards it. Cheapest route to Israel measured at 1.3 kg was EUB at about $25 (15–25 days); the dedicated Israel packet was $30 at 9–18 days; Aramex $41 at 5–8 days. Break-even against buying the same tier retail is around two pieces — below that the postage costs more than the goods save.
